# Make the visual decisions concrete

Use this reference for typography, proportions, colour and composition. Pair it
with the selected visual direction; do not restart the brand or create
unapproved templates. Use [verified award benchmarks](award-benchmarks.md)
for the owner's international-level quality bar. The foundations below support
legibility and judgement; they do not by themselves establish excellent design.

## Establish type roles with actual Chinese content

Choose a page title, section heading, reading text, navigation/action and
supporting-note role before tuning individual elements. Assign size, weight,
line height and measure to each role; change a role because of its reading task,
not merely because one heading failed to fit. Local fonts remain 0 KiB transfer.

Inspect the real system-font fallbacks on each target platform. A declared
650/750 weight may render like the same neighbouring weight; unsupported weights
and synthesis cannot be assumed to create hierarchy. Compare actual ink shape
and contrast. A larger title with a quieter adjacent label often separates roles
better than making every label bold. Keep secondary operational information
readable; colour and tiny text are not substitutes for hierarchy.

Chinese `ch` is not a reliable count of Han glyphs. Use the actual paragraph and
mixed Latin/numeric content to set a comfortable reading width. A short mobile
title can use balanced wrapping as an enhancement, then be inspected manually.
Keep semantic words together where space permits; do not `nowrap` an entire
heading or force fixed `<br>` breaks to repair every viewport. At 200% text,
allow wrapping rather than squeezing the font or clipping the line.

Set the appropriate language, such as `zh-Hant-TW`. Check opening/closing
punctuation, short tail lines and mixed phone/numeric text. Use normal CSS line
breaking first, then make a small composition or wording change while preserving
meaning. Do not blanket-justify short captions or add spaces between Chinese
characters. [Chinese Layout Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/) is a
W3C work-in-progress reference, not a new normative project gate. Its discussion
of punctuation and regional layout is useful; it does not require book-style
justification or one universal web reading width.

## Build proportions from a dominant relationship

Mark the subject/ink area of each owned image, not only its rectangular bounds.
A portrait with transparent margins can look tiny even when its element is large.
Choose the image's role: visual protagonist, supporting evidence, diagram or
quiet background. Its scale, crop and placement should express that role.
Owner-protected whole graphics retain their complete canvas and aspect ratio.

Sketch an alignment axis and a reading axis. They can differ: a photograph may
span the page while the opening copy aligns with the next article's reading
column. Use a coherent grid as a construction tool; do not expose it as equal
cards everywhere. Remove a surface and assess whether grouping still reads.

At 375px, reconstruct those relationships. For a physician, portrait/name can
form a compact opening, then credentials return to full width. For a comparison,
preserve the two-dimensional axis in a labelled scroll region. Stacking desktop
columns with their old widths leaves unnecessary holes and broken words.

Do not optimise every section to the same height. Plot the whole-page rhythm:
where should the reader scan, pause, compare or act? A compact contact summary,
a generous image and a denser reading section can coexist. Use changes of density
and image scale before adding a different coloured box to every section.

## Colour carries roles

Give the retained greens, warm paper, ink and small accents explicit roles.
Use light green for a quiet field or selected surface, dark ink for reading,
and a restrained warm accent for a meaningful visual beat. Do not assign a new
colour to every topic or rely on colour alone for selection/status.

Measure text and focus against their actual neighbours, including intermediate
photo transitions. A palette swatch's contrast says little about lettering over
a photograph. Reduce background detail in the reading area by composition,
position or a deliberate stable surface; do not fade the entire brand image
until its subject disappears. A forest background can connect selected sections
without being stamped behind every paragraph.

## Action design is information design

Differentiate booking, article navigation, in-page movement, image enlargement,
telephone and external transport by their action and label. One repeated
outlined rectangle and a font arrow cannot express all those relationships.
Use an intentional primary action and quieter but substantial navigation rows;
keep each link's hit area at least the project's required size.

Use one SVG stroke/size vocabulary for directional icons. Match the icon to the
action; do not give a same-page anchor an external-link arrow. Preserve an
accessible text label, visible focus and stable hit geometry. A whole-row link
can be useful; do not nest a second action inside it.

## Inspect the actual candidate against strong work

Inspect the clinic candidate beside relevant verified, jury-awarded work at
comparable desktop/mobile widths. Name the specific relationship: title versus
image, name versus portrait, action versus body text, or summary versus full
comparison. Judge its craft against the selected references and clinic brief,
not simply against the previous website. Preserve the clinic's own assets and
facts; a reference relationship is not permission to copy a foreign layout.

A change of colour/radius with the same hierarchy is not a structural
alternative. If composition, type, imagery or motion remains materially weaker
than the relevant reference, report visual FAIL and revise. Do not insert
invented bad/good sample pages into the skill as design authority. Actual
candidate iterations remain task evidence until they meet the stated bar.
