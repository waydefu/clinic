# Concept and direction

How to go from a brief to a direction the owner can choose, and how the
selected direction applies to each page. Values are in
[visual-foundations](visual-foundations.md); review in [visual-review](visual-review.md).

## Write the brief before drawing

One short paragraph per page type: who arrives (often an older adult on a phone,
worried about breathing or sleep), what they must find or do (hours, location,
which service fits, which doctor, booking or a call), what must stay visible,
and what the primary action produces. The brief decides the first screen; a
decorative opening that pushes hours and booking away fails it.

## One governing concept

Award juries reward one idea carried through the whole site. For this clinic:
**breath and airflow, framed by everyday function** — smell, breathe, sleep —
expressed with restraint. Ways it can recur: a single hairline motif reused as
divider and accent, a slow calm rhythm in motion, copy that names functions
rather than diseases, photography of real rooms in soft natural light. Write
the concept in one sentence in the design record and test every later choice
against it. A concept is not a licence for decoration.

## Start from the assets

Inspect the photographs, portraits and illustrations before laying out
sections: subject, negative space, usable crop, light direction. Give owned
assets a strong role through scale and placement instead of fitting them into
a generic grid. Record every derivative crop; keep masters. Generated or
redrawn imagery needs the owner's rights confirmation per file and never
replaces real anatomy, devices or identifiable physicians.

## Directions as style tiles

When the direction is open, show two or three **named** directions as style
tiles: type roles with real Chinese text, colour roles, one image treatment,
one component (for example the booking row) and three adjectives. Structural
differences belong in the first screen and one consequential section; a colour
swap on the same layout is a variation, not a direction. Recommend one with
reasons; record the choice. Do not blend directions on your own. If the owner
asks for a combination, give each direction one job and write it down (on
2026-10-12, A supplied the skeleton: type scale, bleed, numbered sections; B
supplied the texture: rounded white cards, pill buttons, the floating info
card), then apply that split everywhere.

Build tiles as real mini homepages with the clinic's own content and photos,
and score them with the specimen strip hidden (a `?bare` switch): the strip's
type specimens and swatches inflate the font-size count and object count and
pull every tile down the same way.

## Defaults that make a site look generated

- Cards default to no. Start with text, images and space; add a surface only for
  a real grouping or interaction. If removing border, radius, fill and shadow
  keeps the grouping clear, remove them.
- No eyebrow + H2 + subtitle + card-grid recipe repeated down the page, no
  identical rounded images in rows, no decorative pills or numbers, no gradient
  blobs or glass, no uniform centring, no default text-left/image-right hero.
- No English labels over Chinese content unless they carry information.
- Vary density: a compact contact summary, a generous image and a denser
  reading section can sit together.

## Page roles

- **Home:** one opening composition (the clinic's own brand wall photograph),
  then hours/contact/booking, the space, care topics, process, physicians,
  SnoreLab, visit information, FAQ. An editorial sequence, not seven card
  sections. Text never covers the photograph.
- **Doctors:** whole profiles with portrait, name, title, focus and summary;
  one consistent pattern across list and profile pages; no doctor-specific
  booking parameter.
- **Doctor profile:** portrait and name form a compact opening; expertise,
  education and publications as ruled columns of plain lists.
- **Treatment pages:** health education in this order — what it is, causes or
  structure, who should be assessed, the visit, aftercare. Comparisons keep both
  axes (a labelled scroll region on phones). No outcome claims.

## Mobile is another composition

At 375 px reassign the reading order, image crops and the visual axis rather
than stacking desktop columns. Keep 44 px targets, native semantics, visible
focus and readable text at 200%. A crop must never remove the subject or a
required label.
