# Asset-led clinic composition

Apply the selected direction: boutique medical identity, editorial medical
publishing and quiet Japanese spatial character. Retain the mist forest,
light/deep greens, owned drawings, real clinic photographs and physician
portraits. Retaining this world does not require retaining the old layout.

## Start with the assets

Inspect meaningful images before laying out sections. Find their subject,
negative space, usable crop and relationship to the content. Give an owned
asset a stronger role through scale, cross-column placement or foreground and
background relationships. Do not design a generic grid and then fit drawings
into its slots. Preserve masters and record derivative crops.

Selective redraw is allowed when the user has authorised it for weak artwork;
record that exception. It is not blanket authority to replace brand assets
with generated imagery, stock photographs or generic icons. Do not redraw
actual anatomy, devices or identifiable physicians to improve presentation.

Treat an owner-designated complete infographic as a whole composition. Preserve
its text, labels, device edges, comparison relationships and aspect ratio.
Do not extract a nose, device or individual type merely to fit a new layout.
Resize/compress the full canvas; use full-image enlargement and native readable
text when mobile details need more room. The latest asset-specific instruction
overrides an earlier general permission to crop. Record protected graphics in
one preservation manifest and verify rendered bounds against it.

## Cards default to no

Start with text, images and space. Add a surface only when it explains a real
grouping or interaction. If removing its border, radius, fill and shadow leaves
the information clear, remove those treatments.

Avoid repeated eyebrow/H2/subtitle/card sections, identical rounded images,
equal padding, decorative pills and numbers, gradient blobs, glass effects,
uniform centring and the default text-left/image-right hero. Content may need
a genuine grid or table; that is different from using cards to avoid arranging
information.

Choose a dominant visual idea for every page. Let local typography, image
scale/crop and changing density carry the hierarchy. Keep font transfer at
0 KiB. Use plain, natural Traditional Chinese. Source provenance belongs in
the review record, not repetitive patient-facing labels about the old website.

## Page-specific roles

- Homepage: one clinic opening composition, six-photo presentation, compact
  hours/contact, four care topics, physicians, space and transport. Arrange
  these as an editorial sequence rather than seven card sections.
- Snoring: daytime/night-time human experience, then five categories of causes
  and body structure. Do not make five equal cause cards.
- Turbinate: anatomy, a real nine-column comparison and two original devices.
  Preserve comparison across rows/columns, including a usable mobile scroll
  region; do not substitute nine cards.
- Septoplasty: partner drawing, structure and five anatomy types form a
  narrative. Figures can be compared without becoming bordered cards.
- Mouthguard: actual appliance photography leads. Assessment, making,
  adjustment, wearing and care are a real sequence, so ordered treatment is
  appropriate here. Do not invent the missing clinical details.
- Physicians: portrait, name, specialty, experience and care roles form whole
  profiles. No individual booking CTA or doctor-specific booking parameter.

## Mobile is another composition

Reassign the visual axis, reading order and image crop at 375px. Do not merely
stack the desktop columns. Preserve text at 200%, 44px controls, native
semantics and visible keyboard focus. A viewport crop must not become a
missing subject, clipped label or hidden required information.

Motion needs a visible contribution to the composition. Keep the visual anchor
stable as a photograph or reading relationship changes; a faint effect on every
section does not create a motion direction. Keep motion finite, stop it for
reduced motion, and stop autoplay entirely under reduced motion. The owner's
later Anime.js request makes it a candidate for research and measured selection;
it does not itself authorise dependency or website changes. Follow
[ui-motion](../../ui-motion/SKILL.md) for choreography and implementation judgement.
