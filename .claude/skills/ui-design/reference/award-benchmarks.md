# Judge against professionally awarded work

The owner's 2026-10-08 brief sets international design-competition-level craft
as the minimum for the selected clinic redesign. Retain the clinic's existing
brand world; raise composition, typography, imagery, mobile reconstruction and
motion together. A generic page that passes every engineering check still fails
the visual brief. The current website supplies assets and a before-state, not
the success standard. Do not create teaching demos to certify your own design.

This reference records verified awards and bounded observations, not templates,
a claim that our site has won an award, or a promise of a future jury result.
Daily website awards are not annual grand prizes. A jury score establishes the
reference's provenance; it is not a numeric project gate or the maker's score.

## What was inspected and what can serve this clinic

### Clinic Beethovenstrasse: typography carries the spatial structure

[Current site](https://www.colette-camenisch.com/en), inspected on 2026-10-08 at
1440 × 1000 and 375 × 1000, normal motion preference. The clinic name fills the
desktop width; mobile gives it two deliberate lines with a narrow page margin.
The service index uses large type, generous rows and fine separators rather
than a grid of equally sized boxes. This shows how typography can make both a
brand introduction and a useful directory feel like composed space.

For Beau Essence, establish type/image relationships with the actual Chinese
title and local fonts; use a substantial care index only if its reading task
benefits. Do not copy Latin font sizes, the beige palette or foreign fonts.
Its hero video stayed on a black loading spinner in these observations, so the
complete hero and its video pacing remain unverified. The
[creator's case study](https://www.eseagency.ch/en/work/clinic-beethovenstrasse)
reports animated navigation/team treatments; that is attributed description,
not observed playback. Do not import its infinite scrolling or booking model.

### Hyoumankind: a real object organises the brand story

The [creator's published case](https://www.almond.studio/work/hyoumankind)
was read, and its published homepage/product stills were inspected in the
browser at 1440 × 1000. In the homepage still, the pillow appears within the
large scene of a resting person and again as an isolated object in the small
product area. The product still relates the isolated object to an actual use
scene. Scale and repeated subject connect the two views; it is not an arbitrary
collection of decorative images.

Borrow that subject continuity for the clinic's own mouthguard photograph or
environment series. Do not copy the inset rounded image, cutout panel, pills,
commerce, testimonial claims or product-benefit language. The creator reports
a scroll-linked 3D material demonstration; its live timing was not verified.
The [current live site](https://hyoumankind.co.nz/) was also inspected at 1440
and 375, but its opening differs from the archive. It is not evidence that the
current version is the awarded version. The archive supplies a static design
relationship, not a ready-made mobile or animation benchmark.

### Praxis Leandra Isler: a consistent field connects type and photography

[Current site](https://www.leandra-isler.ch/) is a visual reference for the
natural clinic direction, not a medical source. A read-only Luna observation
on 2026-10-08 covered 1440 × 1000 hero/sections and 375 × 812 hero/menu states;
the maker also inspected the resulting stills. The settled hero uses large
asymmetrically placed type over a textured ochre field. The offer photographs
share that field and coordinated lighting, so their objects belong to the same
visual world as the typography. A horizontal drag visibly moved the offer track;
the mobile menu changed to a full-screen text composition with a nested list.

Borrow consistency between owned imagery, surrounding colour and type rhythm,
not its card grid or oversized hero recipe. Reject its multi-second loading
intro, locked document scrolling, essential-copy blur and drag-only discovery.
Do not import its claims or booking assumptions. Timed stills exposed different
states, not smoothness; the recorded WebM was not reviewed end to end and mobile
below-fold content was not sampled. The 2026 observation does not prove parity
with the 2025 awarded version. Award provenance cannot excuse these limitations
or override the clinic's readability, card-default or first-paint requirements.

These observations are limited browser research, not accessibility acceptance
or physical-device testing. Some analytics requests were blocked during the
Beethovenstrasse/Hyoumankind live inspection. No third-party image, font, code
or recording is bundled in the skill. References do not grant asset licences.

## Apply the bar to an actual authorised candidate

Choose the strongest relevant relationship from inspected work and explain why
it serves the clinic. A link collection, matching palette or long award list is
not design research. Use complete owned illustrations, the forest atmosphere,
clinic photos and physician portraits as the raw material of the composition.
Do not first invent an unrelated template and then force the assets into it.
Owner-protected complete medical graphics retain every label and their ratio.

Before coding a treatment, name its dominant idea, title/image relationship,
reading axis, dense/open passages and mobile reconstruction. For motion, name
the stable anchor, trigger, sequence, intermediate/settled states and static
equivalent. A framework name is not a motion direction. Study a normal-mode
interaction through settlement before borrowing its timing; mark unobserved
choreography as a hypothesis. Use [ui-motion](../../ui-motion/SKILL.md).

Review the clinic's real 1440/375 rendering beside the relevant inspected work:

- **Identity and composition:** one visual protagonist belongs to this clinic;
  copy substitution cannot turn it into a generic SaaS/hotel landing page.
- **Type and space:** type roles, alignment and density create a reading rhythm;
  repeated eyebrow/heading/cards or uniform section padding cannot do that work.
- **Imagery:** image scale and foreground/background relationships have intent;
  paid whole graphics are neither cropped nor shrunk into incidental icons.
- **Actions and finish:** navigation, booking, contact and image controls have
  appropriate hierarchy, coherent icon geometry and considered visible states.
- **Mobile:** 375px has a composed first screen and reading order of its own;
  desktop stacking and hidden overflow do not establish mobile craft.
- **Motion:** arrival and interaction preserve attention and continuity through
  intermediate frames; a running carousel or a fade everywhere is insufficient.

For every material gap, record route, viewport/state, element, visible problem,
why it weakens composition and the concrete revision. Inspect at actual reading
size, including action/icon close-ups; a small full-page contact sheet can hide
poor craft. Capture, critique the three weakest structural areas, revise and
render again. Repeat until the homepage meets the brief before extending five
distinct page compositions. See [visual review](visual-review.md).

Technical PASS cannot erase a visual FAIL. Do not rename an unresolved gap as
“minimalism”, claim competition-level work from self-scoring, or lower the bar
because the old website was weaker. Identify self-review as self-review; owner
acceptance and clinical approval remain separate. Reuse validated type/action
roles and controller boundaries while keeping each page's editorial structure.

## Keep the project's boundaries

Awarded work is a reference for craft, not permission to copy assets, invent
medical content or import a different product model. Preserve HTML/CSS/ES
Modules, local fonts with 0 KiB font transfer, native semantics, Trusted Types,
keyboard/focus, 44px targets, 200% text and reduced motion. Maintain the booking
boundary, physician roles, clinic freeze and existing route budgets. Measure
the actual asset allocation and full route closure; do not silently raise a
budget or install a framework to imitate an effect. No deployment follows from
this reference. Resolve visual choices autonomously within the user's authority;
only material authority/content/scope changes need a new owner decision.
