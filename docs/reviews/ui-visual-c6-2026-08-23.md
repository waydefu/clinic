# C6 final runtime visual evidence — 2026-08-23

## Status and boundary

This is the current C6 reference-only visual evidence set. It was captured from
the synthetic browser-local runtime with Chromium, warm theme, `zh-TW`,
Asia/Taipei, reduced motion, DPR 1 and a fixed application clock. It is not a
cross-OS pixel gate and contains no real patient or clinic-calendar data.

The [C4 set](ui-visual-c4-2026-08-22.md), the
[C3 baseline](ui-visual-baseline-2026-08-22.md) and all older dated directories
remain unchanged historical evidence. C6 uses the separate
`ui-visual-c6-2026-08-23` directory specifically to avoid rewriting them.

**There is no C5 visual set.** The C5 runtime
(`a9c525695c2920f82b5b5ead2def576ed8bbd927`) was deployed and then rejected for
final acceptance because its mobile success screen kept
`data-booking-flow-step="3"`, so capture scenario 15 could not reach
`查詢／取消預約` at 375px at all. That failure is the reason C6 exists, and the
seventeenth scenario below exists to keep it from recurring.

## The scenario that is new in C6

Scenario 9 is the required mobile success state. Its capture helper asserts,
before the screenshot is taken, that after a completed mobile booking:

- `data-booking-flow-step` is `result` and no longer `3`;
- `.patient-header` is visible;
- `#booking-management-open`（查詢／取消預約）is visible; and
- the theme control is visible.

The capture pipeline additionally fails the scenario on any horizontal overflow
or any console output, so those checks apply to this screen too.

## Manual inspection

All seventeen captures were opened and inspected. The final pass confirmed:

- the large hero exists only on Step 1, and the synthetic warning and top status
  line are Step-1-only by design while the status line stays in the
  accessibility tree;
- month cards wrap without a 60-day horizontal strip, mobile has no page-level
  horizontal overflow, and empty time groups are not rendered;
- 上午, 中午 and 晚間 use balanced columns and normal-height tokenized controls;
- Steps 2 and 3 use the inline compact panel heading, so the back action and the
  step title share one row;
- Step 3 begins with one back action plus `重新開始`, uses the two actual
  semantic form sections without the removed summary sidebar, and ends with the
  clinic contact block whose text states that social messages may not be
  immediate and never send or change a booking automatically;
- the success screen is a result rather than a fourth step, and on mobile it now
  returns the header, the lookup/cancel entry and the theme control instead of
  stranding the patient;
- the urgent cancellation view makes the phone action primary, displays LINE,
  Instagram, Messenger and Facebook, and states that social messages are not
  immediate and do not automatically cancel the booking;
- the workbench omits normal closed-day columns, adds an explicit `加開` day
  with its hours, removes an explicitly closed normal day, and renders no fake
  events; and
- desktop/mobile Case layouts, dialogs, footer flow, focus-safe controls and
  information hierarchy have no observed clipping, overlap or unintended page
  overflow.

The capture recorded zero console errors and zero console warnings in every
scenario. Patient records and browser-local state are synthetic; the
cancellation and Step 3 contact views use only the already-published clinic
contact details. The evidence contains no Calendar credentials, IDs or event
text.
