## Manual inspection

All sixteen captures were opened and inspected. The final pass confirmed:

- the large hero exists only on Step 1 and the thin synthetic warning persists;
- month cards wrap without a 60-day horizontal strip, mobile has no page-level
  horizontal overflow, and empty time groups are not rendered;
- 上午, 中午 and 晚間 use balanced columns and normal-height tokenized controls;
- Step 3 begins with one back action plus `重新開始`, then uses the two actual
  semantic form sections without the removed summary sidebar or dead quadrant;
- the urgent cancellation view makes the phone action primary, displays LINE,
  Instagram, Messenger and Facebook, and states that social messages are not
  immediate and do not automatically cancel the booking;
- the workbench omits normal closed-day columns, adds an explicit `加開` day
  with its hours, removes an explicitly closed normal day, and renders no fake
  events; and
- desktop/mobile Case layouts, dialogs, footer flow, focus-safe controls and
  information hierarchy have no observed clipping, overlap or unintended page
  overflow.

The capture recorded zero console errors and zero console warnings in every
scenario. Patient records and browser-local state are synthetic; the
cancellation view uses only the already-published clinic contact details. The
evidence contains no Calendar credentials, IDs or event text.
